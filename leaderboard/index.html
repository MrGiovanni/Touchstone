Touchstone Benchmark

A large-scale benchmark for medical image segmentation. AI algorithms are trained on AbdomenAtlas and independently evaluated on out-of-distribution CT from hospitals the models never saw during training.

  • 5,195training CT · 76 hospitals
  • 5,903test CT · 11 hospitals
  • 9anatomical structures
  • 17AI algorithms ranked (of 19 benchmarked)
  • 14research teams

Metric is Dice Similarity Coefficient (DSC), higher is better. Machine-readable copies of this table live in leaderboard/data/ as JSON and CSV.
